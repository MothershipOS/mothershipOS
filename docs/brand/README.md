# mothershipOS brand

The mark is a carrier with a small craft just launched from its bay: one phone that carries and launches other
operating systems. The carrier is a flying wing whose sawtooth trailing edge draws an M; the small craft (a VM) is
the one accent.

| File | What | Use on |
|---|---|---|
| `logo.svg`, `logo-light.svg` | mark + wordmark, horizontal | light / dark backgrounds |
| `mark.svg`, `mark-light.svg` | the mark alone, square, transparent | light / dark backgrounds |
| `icon.svg`, `icon-light.svg` | app icon: rounded square (rx 112 of 512), the small craft enlarged to read at small sizes | anywhere; the dark one is the default |
| `icon-512.png`, `icon-192.png`, `mark-512.png` | the same as PNG, transparent | where SVG is not taken |

| Color | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#1D1D1B` | the carrier and wordmark on light; the icon's background |
| Paper | `#F6F6F4` | the carrier and wordmark on dark |
| Signal orange | `#E8622C` | the small craft and "OS"; never for the carrier |

Wordmark: Space Grotesk (SIL Open Font License 1.1), "mothership" at weight 500 and "OS" at 700, tracking -1 %,
converted to outlines (the SVGs need no font). Keep the mark at least 16 px tall (below that the small craft is
dropped by the renderer, the carrier still reads) and clear space around it of half its height.
